# Fanmade Flash Pixel Game

A 2D pixel-art endless runner similar to *Temple Run* where instead of the monkey chasing ya, it's the Black Flash and playable characters are related to DC's Flash. Made using Python and Pygame.


## Requirements
- Idle python 3
- pygame


## How to play
- Download the zip/files from this repo.
- Click on the maingame.py and select your speedster.


<img width="1060" height="560" alt="Screenshot (323)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bcd56238-0138-45fb-9bf5-5d819fb4cf23" />


- Press D to run, F to fire projectiles and SPACE for jumping/dodging.
- While being chased, Black flash can shoot 3 types of projectiles - basic, double and boomerang. You get hit 4 times, gg it's over.


<img width="1060" height="560" alt="Screenshot (325)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4717195c-3853-4014-a9f0-cba5707a84ec" />


- You can reduce the hit counter by collecting 1 heart for 1 hit. If you managed to collect all 9 hearts, you get a 5x score boost and you enter the Speed-Force - where you are temporarily *title card* Invincible.

  
<img width="1060" height="560" alt="Screenshot (326)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b305bb97-75d2-4a0c-928a-1afb63f91d25" />


- If you don't like the music, you can change it in the file folder by replacing with another ogg file of your choice named "finale.ogg" .
